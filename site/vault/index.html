The media vault

Every video and image, in an encrypted vault

The Reels and images posted from this site are kept in an sgit vault: encrypted, versioned and stored as ciphertext. This is its public read key. It lets anyone open and copy the files, and nothing else: it cannot change, add or delete anything.

Open it in your browser
